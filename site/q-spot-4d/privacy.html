Q Spot 4D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Q Spot 4D (“the app”, Q Spot · Nehroopa 4D Predictions) is a product of the brand Mass tech, India. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What the app is (and is not)

Q Spot 4D shows published 4D / lottery results, number statistics and fun predictions. It is for entertainment and information only. The app does not sell lottery tickets, accept bets or payments, or pay prizes, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any lottery operator. Results are taken from public sources and may contain errors – always check the official operator. The app is for adults aged 18 and over.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address (from your Google account) to create your profile in the app.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a device token is used by Firebase Cloud Messaging to send you “result is out” alerts. You can turn this off in the app or in your phone settings.
  • Content you create: things you post or like in the community features of the app.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising ID and similar device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your phone’s Google settings.
  • Technical data: basic diagnostic and device information (such as app version and device model) handled by the services below.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your profile, deliver result notifications, run the community features, show ads and keep the app working. We do not sell your personal information.

Services that process data

Google Firebase (authentication, cloud messaging, database) and Google AdMob. Their own policies apply: policies.google.com/privacy.

Storage and security

Some app data is stored on your device. Data held in Firebase is protected by access rules and encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above, or if required by law.

Your choices and deletion

You can sign out, turn off notifications and uninstall the app at any time. To have your account data deleted, email nkmasstech@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete it.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
